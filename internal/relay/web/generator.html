Ending finish

Default: fade the final 1 second of the complete sequence to black and silence. The fade is included in total duration, after transition overlaps; it adds no time. Use at least two video frames, or 0 to keep the final image and audio. Generate to inspect the exact finish.

This media plays once. Output draining happens afterward under broadcast control and is separate from media duration. Generating or previewing does not end a broadcast.

Continuous soundtrack

Music starts once at the beginning and continues across scenes. End mode fades at the earlier track/design end; repeat mode fades only at design end. Source-video volume stays separate. If the mix would clip, one fixed gain reduces the whole mix; the applied gain appears in the result. Generate to hear the exact mix.

Complete prestream cycle

The complete scene sequence and mixed soundtrack repeat together. A crossfade overlaps the end with the beginning, shortens the cycle, and starts playback just after that opening overlap. Exact preview plays two passes. Cycle length is not a countdown to going live.

Video uses exact frame timing. AAC audio boundaries have up to one packet of codec granularity (about 21–23 ms); playback keeps the same cycle clock without cumulative drift.
