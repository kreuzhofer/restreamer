Exact prepared output

Design history

Generating and previewing do not select or activate media. Ready revisions are available in live control. Visual sequences support up to 600 seconds at 1920 × 1080 and 30 fps; Follow music exports support up to one hour.

Music & duration

Music continues across scenes. In Fixed mode, End mode fades at the earlier track/design end; Repeat mode fades at design end. Follow music applies fades to each complete music pass and repeats independently of the visual cycle. Source-video volume stays separate. If the mix would clip, one fixed gain reduces the whole mix; the applied gain appears in the result. Generate to hear the exact mix.

Manage tracks and playlists

Appearance is separate from scene content and timing.

Ending finish

Default: fade the final 1 second of the complete sequence to black and silence. The fade is included in total duration, after transition overlaps; it adds no time. Use at least two video frames, or 0 to keep the final image and audio. Generate to inspect the exact finish.

Fixed-duration endings play once and stop the stream after delivery drains. Follow music repeats the visual sequence and music independently until you stop or change stage. Generating and previewing never change playback.

Complete prestream cycle

In fixed mode, the scene sequence and mixed soundtrack repeat together. With Follow music, the complete visual and music loops repeat independently at their original speed. A crossfade overlaps the end with the beginning, shortens the cycle, and starts playback just after that opening overlap. Fixed-mode preview plays two visual cycles; Follow music preview plays one music pass. Cycle length is not a countdown to going live.

Video uses exact frame timing. AAC audio boundaries have up to one packet of codec granularity (about 21–23 ms); playback keeps the same cycle clock without cumulative drift.

Scene actions

Selecting a new video starts at its source duration. Shorten the scene or enable repetition explicitly; only the selected range repeats. Audio starts muted.

Images keep their proportions and transparency. Text-with-image splits the centered content region into two columns. Full-screen media fills the frame with the complete image; custom content dimensions can inset it.

Line breaks are preserved. Go fonts preserve mixed case and support Latin (including accented European text), Greek and Cyrillic. Arcade Pixel renders uppercase A–Z, digits, German umlauts and basic punctuation. Unsupported characters and overflow are shown below. Up to 4096 UTF-8 bytes.
